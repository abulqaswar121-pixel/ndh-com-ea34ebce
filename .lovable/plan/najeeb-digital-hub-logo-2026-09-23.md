# Najeeb Digital Hub logo

## Chosen direction
Create a **Tech-Forward ND Monogram**: a distinctive geometric symbol that joins “N” and “D” into one continuous, forward-moving form. This is the strongest fit because NDH combines digital services and technology education, while the symbol remains clear in a website header, profile image, app icon, and favicon.

## Logo creation
- Develop two genuinely different executions of the ND monogram—not mere colour variations.
- Use the established NDH palette: deep navy `#0A1A30`, purple `#8A2BE2`, magenta `#FF007F`, cyan `#22D3EE`, and white.
- Pair the symbol with the exact name **NAJEEB DIGITAL HUB**, using clean geometric lettering with deliberate spacing.
- Keep the artwork isolated with generous margins and transparent background; no mockup, scenery, slogan, extra wording, or checkerboard.
- Preserve a strong silhouette and simple interior spacing so the symbol remains recognizable at favicon size.

## Selection and refinement
- Inspect both concepts for exact lettering, balance, clipping, transparency, and readability on both navy and white backgrounds.
- Choose the strongest concept without requiring another decision from you.
- Refine that chosen image rather than regenerating from scratch if spacing, lettering, or edges need correction.
- Deliver the final transparent PNG logo plus a symbol-only PNG for small placements. This task will not change the website yet.
