# Precision Gateway redesign and reliability pass

## Direction
Use the selected **Precision Gateway Grid** as the shared visual system: Space Grotesk headings, DM Sans body text, deep ink navy, pure white, porcelain, signal cyan and controlled violet. Preserve the approved Open Gateway symbol and keep the homepage as the independent parent-brand gateway.

## What will change
- Recompose the homepage into a tighter gateway experience with a stronger dark opening, useful quick paths, a clearer business directory, credible family proof, live/coming status and a more purposeful final action.
- Bring Agency, Academy, Work, Blog, About, Contact, verification and legal pages into the same visual family through shared headers, page openings, section bands, cards, buttons and typography.
- Restyle all five signed-in portals with the same precise shell, navigation, page headers, status cards and phone-safe layouts without changing role permissions or workflows.
- Improve the family menu, footer and AI assistant placement so they feel integrated rather than floating additions.
- Remove excessive empty space, weak borders, inconsistent corner shapes and oversized mobile typography while preserving readable breathing room.

## Necessary corrections
- Repair the Project monitoring findings affecting the admin review queue, transactional emails, support-chat Enter behaviour and lesson completion integrity.
- Check the remaining monitoring finding and resolve it if reproducible.
- Verify public pages at phone and desktop sizes, then verify available signed-in portal views without inventing account data.
- Confirm every content route has complete, unique search and sharing metadata.

## Scope safeguards
- Use only real NDH business descriptions, counts, links, case studies and operational states.
- Do not fabricate live analytics, investment returns, testimonials or service availability.
- Keep each subsidiary as a sibling destination and retain the catalogue fail-open behaviour on the parent homepage.
- Do not publish until the refreshed preview has been reviewed and approved.

## Technical details
- Consolidate shared visual rules in the existing design tokens and reusable page/portal shells instead of creating page-specific themes.
- Keep current TanStack routing, Lovable Cloud security boundaries and existing business logic.
- Use lightweight CSS motion with reduced-motion fallbacks; avoid animation or media that slows first load.
